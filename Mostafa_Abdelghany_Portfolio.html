About

I lead procurement for one of Saudi Arabia's most complex Vision 2030 mega-projects — and I built the expertise to get here step by step, not overnight. Over 12+ years across Saudi Arabia and Egypt, I progressed from Junior Procurement Engineer to MEP Procurement Section Head, taking on a larger supplier network and team at every stage.

Today I lead Mechanical & Electrical procurement for King Abdullah International Gardens — a SAR 2.63+ billion botanical mega-project spanning 2+ million m² for Riyadh Municipality — managing a procurement team of 3 and a network of 50+ qualified suppliers. This year, I raised material submittal/approval throughput to 25 items per month by sourcing faster-delivery alternatives, protecting the project's critical path.

I'm a Registered Engineer with the Saudi Council of Engineers and hold a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Benha University — a technical foundation that shapes how I evaluate vendors, specifications, and cost variations.
